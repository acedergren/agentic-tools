# Revision Patterns

Use these patterns as prompts for judgment. They are not replacements for context.

## English Source Shape to Swedish Shape

| Source pressure | Swedish move |
| --- | --- |
| Noun stack | Turn part of it into a verb or clause, or use one clear compound. |
| Over-explicit transitions | Omit or simplify when Swedish flow already shows the relation. |
| `we are able to` | Usually `vi kan`; use `har möjlighet att` only when ability/opportunity is the point. |
| `make sure` | `se till att` in everyday/professional text; `säkerställ` in instructions/control contexts. |
| `leverage` | Often `använda` or `dra nytta av`; avoid inflated `utnyttja` unless exploitation/efficient use is intended. |
| `support` | Choose by meaning: `stödja`, `ge stöd för`, `ha stöd för`, `fungera med`, `hjälpa`. |
| `currently` | `just nu`, `för närvarande`, `i dag`, or omit if time is obvious. |
| `take action` | `agera`, `vidta åtgärder`, or a concrete verb. |
| `please find attached` | `Jag bifogar...` or `Bifogat finns...`. |

## Word Order

- Main clause: `I dag lanserar vi funktionen.`
- Not natural: `I dag vi lanserar funktionen.`
- Subordinate clause: `... eftersom vi inte har fått svar.`
- Not natural: `... eftersom vi har inte fått svar.`

## Definiteness

- `en ny funktion` = a new feature
- `funktionen` = the feature
- `den nya funktionen` = the new feature
- `vår nya funktion` = our new feature

If English says `the new feature`, do not automatically use `den nya funktionen`; choose based on whether the feature is already known in the Swedish context.

## Compounds

Prefer true compounds when they are normal:

- `kostnadsanalys`
- `språkstöd`
- `kunddata`
- `migreringsplan`

Rephrase heavy compounds:

- Heavy: `kunddatamigreringsriskanalys`
- Better: `analys av risker i kunddatamigreringen`

## Professional Email Defaults

- Greeting: `Hej Anna,` or `Hej,`
- Neutral close: `Vänliga hälsningar`
- Warmer close: `Hälsningar`
- Very formal close: `Med vänliga hälsningar`

Avoid adding elaborate politeness unless the relationship or medium calls for it.

## Common Polish Moves

- Replace repeated `det finns` with a concrete subject when possible.
- Replace `göra en analys av` with `analysera` unless the noun phrase is needed.
- Replace `ha en påverkan på` with `påverka` when the meaning is simple.
- Replace vague `saker` with a concrete noun, or keep `saker` if the voice is deliberately casual.
- Keep one strong verb instead of adding adverbs that compensate for weak phrasing.
