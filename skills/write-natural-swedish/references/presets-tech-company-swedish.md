# Presets for Contemporary and Tech-Company Swedish

Use these presets when the user asks for modern Swedish, technology-company writing, developer-facing text, product copy, or workplace communication in a tech context.

## Contemporary

Goal: sound current without sounding forced.

- Prefer words that are already normal in Swedish usage: `app`, `mejl`, `sajt`, `webbplats`, `chattbott`, `AI`, `molntjänst`, `plattform`, `integration`, `data`, `funktion`.
- Use new words only when they genuinely name the thing. `AI-agent`, `digital suveränitet`, and `vibbkodning` can be right in current tech contexts; they should not be sprinkled into ordinary copy for freshness.
- Keep English terms when they are product names, protocol/API names, command names, UI strings, or the established term in the target company.
- Translate concepts, not vibes. `vibe coding` may become `vibbkodning` in trend/reporting contexts, but `AI-stödd programmering` or `programmering med AI` may be clearer in enterprise or customer copy.
- Avoid faux-modern filler: `superladda`, `disrupta`, `gamechanger`, `next-gen`, `sömlös` as a reflex, and English adjective order copied into Swedish.

## Tech Company

Goal: sound like a credible Swedish tech company.

- Be concrete before enthusiastic: say what the product does, who it helps, and what changes for the user.
- Use calm confidence. Swedish business tech copy often works better with evidence and clarity than with big claims.
- Prefer `du` for product UI, support, documentation, and most SaaS copy. Use `ni` only for the customer organization when the text clearly addresses a company as a group.
- Prefer verbs over abstractions: `automatisera`, `skydda`, `samla`, `analysera`, `prioritera`, `publicera`, `felsöka`, `integrera`.
- Keep English product and engineering terms when Swedish alternatives sound unnatural to the audience, but explain them when writing for mixed technical/business readers.
- Use Swedish compounds carefully: `molnkostnader`, `säkerhetsgranskning`, `driftstatus`, `kunddata`, `produktteam`, `lanseringskandidat`, `API-integration`.
- Avoid literal English business phrasing: `driva värde`, `på skala`, `ta action`, `låsa upp potential`, `flytta nålen`.

## Developer Docs

Goal: make Swedish technical instructions easy to follow.

- Use direct instructions: `Skapa en ny nyckel`, `Kör kommandot`, `Lägg till variabeln`.
- Put conditions before actions: `Om bygget misslyckas, kör...`
- Keep code, CLI flags, env vars, HTTP methods, endpoint paths, UI labels, and product names unchanged.
- Use consistent terms across the page. Do not alternate between `driftsätta`, `deploya`, and `publicera` unless the distinction matters.
- Prefer present tense and active voice: `API:t returnerar...`, `Tjänsten skapar...`
- Avoid unnecessary politeness in steps. Use `Klicka på Spara`, not `Vänligen klicka på Spara`.
- Explain acronyms on first use for mixed audiences: `rollbaserad åtkomstkontroll (RBAC)`.

## Product Marketing

Goal: Swedish product copy that is persuasive without sounding imported.

- Lead with the user outcome, then the mechanism.
- Make claims testable: `minskar manuell handpåläggning`, `samlar kostnadsdata`, `visar avvikelser i realtid`.
- Prefer Swedish benefit verbs over English launch language: `kom igång`, `få överblick`, `upptäck risker`, `prioritera åtgärder`.
- Use `smart`, `enkel`, `snabb`, and `säker` only when the surrounding sentence proves the claim.
- Avoid exclamation marks, inflated adjectives, and generic `framtidens...` unless the requested tone is explicitly campaign-like.

## Startup Internal

Goal: natural workplace Swedish inside a product/engineering team.

- Keep it direct and warm: `Kan du skicka testresultaten i dag?`
- Use a little shared tech vocabulary if the team would: `release`, `PR`, `deploy`, `rollback`, `feature flag`.
- Keep Swedish grammar around English terms: `deployen`, `PR:en`, `feature flaggen` only in informal team contexts; use clearer rephrasing in external copy.
- Prefer `risker som återstår`, `blockerar lanseringen`, `behöver verifieras`, `nästa steg` over literal English project phrasing.

## Term Choice Heuristics

| English | Usually good Swedish | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dashboard | `instrumentpanel`, `översikt`, `vy`, sometimes `dashboard` | For SaaS UI, `dashboard` can be fine internally; for customer/public copy, `översikt` is often smoother. |
| release candidate | `lanseringskandidat`, `release candidate` | Use Swedish in prose; keep English if it is the team's formal process term. |
| deploy | `driftsätta`, `publicera`, `rulla ut`, `deploya` | Match audience. `deploya` is natural internally, less so in polished customer copy. |
| sync | `synka`, `synkronisera`, `uppdatera` | `Synka` is modern/informal; `synkronisera` is safer in docs. |
| insights | `insikter`, `underlag`, `överblick`, `mönster` | Avoid reflexive `insikter` if `underlag` is clearer. |
| seamless | `smidig`, `utan avbrott`, `utan extra steg` | Avoid overusing `sömlös`. |
| scalable | `skalbar` | Good technical term; explain outcome for business readers. |
| observability | `observabilitet` | Established in technical contexts; explain for mixed audiences. |
| governance | `styrning`, `kontroll`, `governance` | Choose by domain; `governance` may be retained in enterprise frameworks. |
| guardrails | `skyddsräcken`, `ramar`, `begränsningar` | `Skyddsräcken` can sound translated; `ramar` is often smoother. |
